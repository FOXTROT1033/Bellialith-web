Commands

Explore the main commands available in Bellialith.

/roll

Use a roll to discover a character.

/claim

Claim a character available to you.

/profile

View your Bellialith profile, progress and rolls.

/collection

View your collected characters.

/inventory

View items currently in your inventory.

/shop

Browse the Bellialith shop by category.

/buy

Purchase an available shop item.

/sell

Sell a character from your inventory.

/sell all

Sell all sellable characters at once.

/use

Use an item from your inventory.

/daily

Claim the available daily reward.

/botprofile

View Bellialith's status, version and statistics.

/help

View available commands and guidance.
